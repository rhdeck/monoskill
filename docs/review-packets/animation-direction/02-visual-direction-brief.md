# Monoskill hero: visual-direction brief

## Locked creative intent

**Audience:** Agent users who already understand context windows and skill discovery.

**One message:** Too many individually installed skills flood discovery context; one Monoskill preserves every capability with radically less context.

**Platform:** Responsive landing-page hero, silent, looping only after the story is understandable in a still frame.

**Mood:** Abundant, magnetic, clarifying.

**Brand cues:** Ink-black field, warm paper, State Change orange, disciplined condensed typography, visible craft, no generic glassmorphism or purple SaaS gradients.

**Must show:** 47 real skill names; Monoskill visible from the first frame; a continuous physical transformation; 32,817 to 331 discovery characters; the final context reduction.

**Must avoid:** Decorative particles, lines leading nowhere, simultaneous unrelated movement, unreadable shrinking labels, teleportation, a Monoskill object that appears only at the end, and “minimal” empty space without composition.

## Motion language

- **Tone cell:** Calm-sharp with one kinetic compression beat.
- **Hero:** The persistent Monoskill object.
- **Support:** The skill collection and the context-load measurement.
- **Texture:** Static grid, water, paper registration, or orbit rings depending on the selected direction.
- **Easing family:** Emphasized deceleration for arrivals; accelerating exit only for skills entering Monoskill.
- **Base timing unit:** 0.4 seconds.
- **Transition family:** Physical continuity and match movement only. No wipes or scene fades.
- **Stagger:** 60ms groups, never 47 independent random starts.
- **Restraint:** Headline, metric labels, and final Monoskill anchor remain fixed. Only the collection and its context field transform.
- **Wow moment:** The instant the last grouped tranche crosses into Monoskill and the context field drops to its final resting level.

## Treatments

### 01 · Context pressure vessel

The literal flood becomes an engineered chamber. The skill field creates visible pressure; Monoskill is a large orange release valve already attached to the chamber. This is the most direct continuation of the original metaphor and provides the clearest physical cause and effect.

### 02 · Editorial compression press

The context window becomes a crowded printed page. All 47 skill names are typographic matter. A physical press binds them into one orange Monoskill volume while whitespace returns to the page. This is the most distinctive graphic system and best expresses packaging rather than deletion.

### 03 · Context gravity

The provider collection forms concentric context rings around a persistent Monoskill core. The rings and labels collapse inward while the center remains fixed. This is the most cinematic direction, but it risks reading as generic technology unless the typography and measurement remain dominant.
