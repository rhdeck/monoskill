# Monoskill YouTube thumbnail brief

## Concept

Many scattered skill cards are pulled by bright orange threads into one physical `SKILL.md` package. The image should feel like compression with receipts—not a generic AI network.

## Text

**MANY SKILLS**  
**ONE WAY IN**

Keep the two phrases large enough to read at phone size. `ONE WAY IN` is the orange emphasis.

## Composition

- 16:9 YouTube thumbnail.
- Left third: the two-line headline.
- Center: six small labeled skill fragments converging through orange lines.
- Right third: one warm off-white package marked `SKILL.md`, slightly rotated and tactile.
- Dark near-black purple background, off-white typography, State Change orange thread color.
- No face, robot, brain, circuit-board texture, gradients behind the text, tiny decorative copy, or YouTube logo.
